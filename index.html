About MTG Arena EV Calculator

Open-source event expected value & payout analysis

A fast, client-side calculator to model and evaluate expected returns, payout distributions, and break-even win rates for Magic: The Gathering Arena events.

Accurate EV Engine

Calculates exact probability distributions for Single-game (Bo1) and Best-of-3 (Bo3) match formats.

Custom Valuations

Set custom USD values for Gems, Gold, Booster Packs, Play-In Points, and Qualifier Badges.

MTG Land Themes

6 land mana themes (Plains, Island, Swamp, Mountain, Forest, Artifact) + random shuffle.

Auto-Saved Storage

All customizations, presets, and preferences are automatically stored in your browser cache.
